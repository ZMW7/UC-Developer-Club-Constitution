\title{Constitution of \clubName}
\date{}
\maketitle

\constitutionarticle{Name of the Club}

The name of the club shall be \clubName.

\constitutionarticle{Purpose}

The purpose of the \clubName is to give students a collaborative, skill enhancing, and helpful outlet of their skills through software based projects.

\constitutionarticle{Membership}

\section{} There shall be three ($3$) classes of members: voting members, nonvoting members, and honorary members.
\subsection{} Voting membership shall be open to any full-time or part-time undergraduate or graduate students, enrolled in any of the colleges, schools, or divisions of the University at the time of applying for membership. The body of voting members is to be named the \votingBody. Upon graduation or other cessation of enrollment to the University, a member of the \votingBody shall be stripped of their voting membership.
\subsection{} Nonvoting membership shall be open to any student and any member of the University faculty, community or administrative staff.
\subsection{} Honorary membership shall be open to any desiring person, elected to such membership by a majority of the \votingBody.

\section{} There shall be no limit to the size of the membership body of the \shortenedClubName.

\constitutionarticle{Officers}

\section{} 
The Officers of the Student Organization shall be President, Vice President, Treasurer, Outreach Coordinator, and Social Media Manager.
Together, these Officers shall make up the \officersGroup.

\section{} 
Holders of officer positions shall be elected anually by the \votingBody. No Officer shall hold a specific officer position for more than one \electionTerm. No Officer shall hold more than one officer position in a single \electionTerm.

% The President
\section{} 
The President shall be the Primary Officer of the Registered Student Organization, and shall preside over all meetings of \clubName.

\subsection{}
A member of the \votingBody can only be elected to the position of President if they have previously served as an Officer of \clubName, or if they have run for the seat of President in at least two prior elections of \clubName. If these criteria are not met by any members of the club willing to pursue the presidential seat, any member of the \votingBody may be elected to the seat of President.

% Officers Conditions & Removal
\section{} 
An Officer must be matriculated student and be in good standing with their college. Officers who do not meet eligibility requirements must relinquish their position immediately upon notification from the University that they do not meet the eligibility requirements. Ineligible officers will be replaced according to this Constitution.

\section{}
An officer may be removed from their seat through a vote of $80\%$ of all officers and $50\%$ of the \votingBody that is present at a given \meeting, or $60\%$ of all officers and two thirds ($\sfrac{2}{3}$) of the \votingBody that is present at a given \meeting.
A vote to remove a particular officer from their seat may only be held once per term.

\subsection{}
Upon the removal of an Officer, a Special Election shall be held to find a replacement. At least one weeks notice must be given to the \votingBody prior to the Special Election being held. This replacement shall serve as the removed Officer's replacement until the next election. The replacement's service shall not be counted as a full \electionTerm unless their time spent serving as a replacement to the removed Officer exceeds 20 weeks.

\constitutionarticle{Meetings}

\section{} 
\meetings shall occur every Monday and Wednesday during the Fall and Spring undergraduate semesters of the University, excepting holidays and other dates decided by the \officersGroup.

\constitutionarticle{University Advisor}

The Advisor for \clubName must be a member of the University staff or faculty.

\constitutionarticle{Disolution}

The club can be disolved through the unanimous vote of all club officers, as well as a unanimous, save for a maximum number of members that is one less than the number of officer positions as defined by this Constitution, vote by the \votingBody.
In the case of dissolution, funds shall be transfered to an organization with a similar goal, and all essential investments must be returned to the \centerForStudentInvolvement.
The Advisor and the \officersGroup shall oversee the dissolution process.

\constitutionarticle{Amendments}

\section{} 
Amendments to this constitution may be proposed by any member of the \votingBody. 
\subsection{}
Following the proposal of an amendment, a six day waiting period shall begin, and notice of the proposed amendment must be given to the \votingBody within this waiting period. Proposed amendments shall be voted upon by members of the \votingBody in the next \meeting to occur after the waiting period has expired. 

\section{} Amendments may be ratified through a simple majority vote of the \votingBody in conjunction with a $\sfrac{2}{3}$ affirmative vote by the club officers, through a $\sfrac{5}{8}$ affirmative vote of present members of the \votingBody in conjunction with the agreement of one officer, or through a $\sfrac{2}{3}$ affirmative vote of the present members of the \votingBody.

\constitutionarticle{Ratification}

\section{} A supermajority vote of $\sfrac{2}{3}$ of the \votingBody shall be sufficient for the ratification of this constitution, with approval of the appropriate governing board.

\section{Officers and Duties}

% The President
\subsection{President}
The President shall be responsible for establishing the annual goals and path of the club, in accordance with the club's mission.
The president must maintain a Grade Point Average of at least 2.5.

% The Vice President
\subsection{Vice President}
The Vice President shall preside over all \meetings in the absence of the President. The Vice President shall also perform other duties assigned to them by the \officersGroup. The Vice President shall keep the records of membership and the minutes of meetings of the \clubName and the \officersGroup.

% The Treasurer
\subsection{Treasurer} 
The Treasurer shall be responsible for the creation of reports to be shared at the bedhest of the President or the \officersGroup, and shall be responsible for the creation and maintenance of a budget. The Treasurer shall have final approval of all Student Organization expenditures. The Treasurer shall sign all checks in payment of bills. 

\subsubsection{} In the absence of the President and Vice President, the Treasurer shall preside at all meetings of \clubName.
\subsubsection{} The Treasurer shall be responsible for educating their successor on the obligations and responsibility of the Treasurer position.
\subsubsection{} The Treasurer must abide by all Policies and Guidelines set down by their respective Funding Board (Undergraduate Funding Board, Graduate Student Association, Medical Student Association, or Student Bar Association).
\subsubsection{} The Treasurer must keep accounts and records which, at all times, must be open to inspection by the \officersGroup or any authorized auditor.

% Outreach Coordinator
\subsection{Outreach Coordinator}
The Outreach Coordinator shall be responsible for establishing and maintaining partnerships with entities in the community.

% Social Media Manager
\subsection{Social Media Manager}
The Social Media Manager shall be responsible for creating and maintaining official social media channels for the \clubName.

\section{University Advisor}
The University Advisor shall consult with the \clubName and ensure that its activities are consistent with the purpose established by this Constitution. The University Advisor shall meet with the \officersGroup at least twice each semester. The advisor shall serve as the primary signee for the \clubName's off campus bank account.

\section{Project Teams}

There shall be a number of Project Slots, defined by the \officersGroup, with each Project Slot being occupied by a Project Team and its respective Project. When a Project Slot opens up, a \meeting shall be designated a Project Goal Meeting. Any member who is not already a Project Lead may give a Project Pitch at a Project Goal Meeting, proposing a project that they would like to see adopted as a project of the \clubName. The collection of Project Pitches for projects to fill a Project Slot shall be known as a Pitching Round. Following the conclusion of a Pitching Round, members of the \votingBody shall vote for the project that the \clubName shall adopt, with the winning Project taking up a single Project Slot. The member who gave the Project Pitch of the chosen Project shall become Project Lead, or may appoint another member to be Project Lead in their stead.

\subsection{} The Project Lead shall be responsible for organizing and leading the progress of their respective Project. They shall be responsible for giving reports at the request of the \officersGroup.

\subsection{} 

The Project Lead may designate a fellow Project Team member as a Project Lead in absentia.

\subsection{}

The President shall have the power to veto any and all projects, with the exception that they shall be required to accept at least one project per pitching round. The President has the power to bar a Project Pitch from being made.

\subsection{}

A Project Pitch shall be made for a proposed Project, regardless of the number of other proposals made in the same Pitching Round.

\subsection{}

The requirements for a Project's retirement are to be decided through a joint decision made by the members of the project team and the President, or through the members of the project team and an Officer of the President's choosing.

\section{Non-Hazing and Anti-Discrimination Clause}

\subsection{}
The laws of the State of Ohio and the policies of the University concerning hazing shall be observed.

\subsection{}
The \clubName and its members will not engage in hazing. ``Hazing'' means doing any act or coercing another, including the victim, to do any act of initiation into any student or other organization or any act to continue or reinstate membership in or affiliation with any student or other organization that causes or creates a substantial risk of causing mental or physical harm to any person, including coercing another to consume alcohol or a drug of abuse, as defined in section 3719.011 of the Revised Code.

\subsection{}
The University prohibits discrimination and harassment in any of its  activities, programs, admissions, or employment on the basis of age, ancestry, color, disability,  gender identity and/or expression, genetic information, military status (including veteran status),  national origin (including shared ancestry or ethnic characteristics), parental status (including  status as a nursing mother and status as a foster parent), pregnancy, race, religion, sex, sexual  orientation, or any other status protected by law (each a ``protected class'').
